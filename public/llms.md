## About GetYourStay

GetYourStay is a platform for finding verified PG (Paying Guest) accommodations in Bangalore and other major Indian cities. The platform connects tenants with PG owners, offering transparent pricing, verified listings, and modern amenities.

---

## Core Features

- **Verified PG listings** with authentic reviews
- **Zero brokerage** - direct owner-tenant connection
- **Advanced filtering** by area, price, gender, and amenities
- **PG Management App** for property owners
- **Real-time availability** and pricing
- **Secure payment** processing
- **24/7 customer support**

---

## Target Audience

- Working professionals in Bangalore
- College students looking for accommodation
